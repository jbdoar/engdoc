* General Instructions

Record calibrated equipment, configuration, environmental conditions, and
evidence references in the preserved execution workbook. Deviations require a
clear disposition and approval before acceptance.

* Functional Acceptance

** Startup sequence test
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ATP-SYS-001
:STATUS: DRAFT
:OWNER: Test Engineering
:STAGES: FAT SAT
:REQUIREMENTS: REQ-SYS-001
:DESIGN_ITEMS: DES-SYS-001
:EQUIPMENT: EQ-DMM-001
:ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA: The system completes startup within 30 seconds without faults.
:END:

Configure the system in its approved baseline configuration. Initiate startup,
measure elapsed time, record observed states, and retain the event log as
evidence.

* Installation Acceptance

** Installation conformance inspection
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ATP-INST-001
:STATUS: DRAFT
:OWNER: Field Engineering
:STAGES: SAT
:REQUIREMENTS: REQ-INST-001
:DESIGN_ITEMS: DES-ELEC-001
:EQUIPMENT: EQ-DMM-001
:ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA: All installed interfaces conform to approved drawings and are labeled.
:END:

Inspect mechanical, electrical, facility, and safety interfaces against the
approved installation package. Record discrepancies and photographic evidence.

* Performance Acceptance

** Performance verification
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: ATP-PERF-001
:STATUS: DRAFT
:OWNER: Test Engineering
:STAGES: FAT SAT
:REQUIREMENTS: REQ-PERF-001
:DESIGN_ITEMS: DES-MECH-001
:EQUIPMENT: EQ-DMM-001 EQ-DIM-001
:ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA: Measured performance satisfies every specified limit.
:END:

Operate the system through the defined performance test points. Record measured
values, uncertainty, applicable limits, and evidence references.
