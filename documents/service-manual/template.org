* Safety and Service Conditions

Only qualified personnel may service the system. Apply approved isolation,
stored-energy control, and verification procedures before accessing hazards.

* Preventive Maintenance

** Inspect protective functions
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: SVC-SAFE-001
:STATUS: DRAFT
:TASK_TYPE: INSPECTION
:OWNER: Service Engineering
:SKILL_LEVEL: QUALIFIED TECHNICIAN
:INTERVAL_DAYS: 180
:REQUIREMENTS: REQ-SYS-001
:DESIGN_ITEMS: DES-SYS-001
:HAZARDS: HAZ-MECH-001
:EQUIPMENT: EQ-DMM-001
:ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA: Protective functions operate correctly with no damage, bypass, or unresolved fault.
:END:

Isolate hazardous energy, inspect guards and interlocks, exercise each
protective function, and retain observations and evidence.

** Verify mechanical condition
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: SVC-MECH-001
:STATUS: DRAFT
:TASK_TYPE: PREVENTIVE
:OWNER: Mechanical Engineering
:SKILL_LEVEL: QUALIFIED TECHNICIAN
:INTERVAL_DAYS: 365
:REQUIREMENTS: REQ-PERF-001
:DESIGN_ITEMS: DES-MECH-001
:HAZARDS: HAZ-PERF-001
:EQUIPMENT: EQ-DIM-001
:ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA: Alignment, wear, fasteners, and critical dimensions remain within approved limits.
:END:

Inspect mechanical condition, measure critical dimensions, correct deficiencies,
and document parts replaced and as-left measurements.

* Diagnostics

** Diagnose startup fault
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: SVC-DIAG-001
:STATUS: DRAFT
:TASK_TYPE: DIAGNOSTIC
:OWNER: Service Engineering
:SKILL_LEVEL: ENGINEER
:INTERVAL_DAYS:
:REQUIREMENTS: REQ-SYS-001
:DESIGN_ITEMS: DES-SYS-001
:HAZARDS: HAZ-MECH-001
:EQUIPMENT: EQ-DMM-001
:ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA: Root cause is identified and the restored system passes the approved startup test.

Capture configuration and fault evidence before modification. Follow the
approved diagnostic sequence, document the root cause, and repeat acceptance
testing after repair.
