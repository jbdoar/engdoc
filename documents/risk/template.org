* Risk Method

Score each hazard using the approved five-by-five severity and likelihood
matrix. Record controls as controlled requirements, design items, and objective
acceptance procedures. Acceptance of residual risk requires documented owner
approval outside this generated analysis.

hazards. Controls include deterministic startup, guarded operating states,
fault handling, and verification of the approved startup sequence.
