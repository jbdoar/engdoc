* About This Manual

This manual describes intended use, limitations, safety information, normal
operation, recovery, and operator care. Service beyond explicitly identified
operator care must be performed by qualified service personnel.

* Safety

** Safety responsibilities and limitations

:CUSTOM_ID: USR-SAFE-001
:STATUS: DRAFT
:TOPIC_TYPE: SAFETY
:AUDIENCE: OPERATOR
:REQUIREMENTS: REQ-SYS-001

Operate only within approved limits. Do not bypass safeguards. Stop operation
and contact qualified service personnel when protective functions, guards, or
interfaces are damaged or behave unexpectedly.

* Shutdown and Recovery

** Controlled shutdown and fault response
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: USR-STOP-001
:STATUS: DRAFT

:AUDIENCE: OPERATOR

restart when the cause is not understood.
