* Preventive Maintenance

** Inspect protective functions
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: SVC-SAFE-001
:STATUS: DRAFT
:TASK_TYPE: INSPECTION
:OWNER: Service Engineering
:SKILL_LEVEL: QUALIFIED TECHNICIAN
:INTERVAL_DAYS: 180
:REQUIREMENTS: REQ-SYS-001
:DESIGN_ITEMS: DES-SYS-001
:HAZARDS: HAZ-MECH-001
:EQUIPMENT: EQ-DMM-001
:ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA: Protective functions operate correctly with no bypass or unresolved fault.
:END:

Isolate hazardous energy, inspect safeguards, exercise each protective
function, and retain observations and evidence.

** Verify mechanical condition
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: SVC-MECH-001
:STATUS: DRAFT
:TASK_TYPE: PREVENTIVE
:OWNER: Mechanical Engineering
:SKILL_LEVEL: QUALIFIED TECHNICIAN
:INTERVAL_DAYS: 365
:REQUIREMENTS: REQ-PERF-001
:DESIGN_ITEMS: DES-MECH-001
:HAZARDS: HAZ-PERF-001
:EQUIPMENT: EQ-DIM-001
:ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA: Critical mechanical conditions remain within approved limits.
:END:

Inspect wear, alignment, fasteners, and critical dimensions and document the
as-left condition.
